Our Story

Pardesi Consultancy was founded by Sahil Vashisht and Pooja Tiwari, not as a business idea, but as a result of lived experience.

Like many newcomers, we went through the same uncertainty — understanding processes, preparing documents, dealing with timelines, and trying to make the right decisions without clear guidance. We experienced firsthand how overwhelming it can be when information is scattered and advice is unclear.

Every step of our journey required patience, research, and learning from real situations. We understood the importance of doing things correctly the first time, and how even small mistakes could cause unnecessary delays and stress.

Over time, we gained a deep understanding of the system through our own efforts. That experience shaped the foundation of Pardesi Consultancy — a place built to offer honest guidance, clear explanations, and structured support.

Our goal is simple: to help others avoid confusion and move forward with confidence, using knowledge gained from real-life experience, not assumptions.
