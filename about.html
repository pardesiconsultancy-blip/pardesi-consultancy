Our Story

Pardesi Consultancy was founded by Sahil Vashisht and Pooja Tiwari — not as a business plan, but as a result of real-life experience.

Like many newcomers, we faced uncertainty, confusion, and countless questions. Understanding processes, preparing documents, and making the right decisions without clear guidance was challenging and often stressful.

Through our own journey, we learned how important accuracy, timing, and clarity are. Even small mistakes can cause delays, unnecessary stress, and lost opportunities.

Over time, we gained deep insight through research, effort, and lived experience. That journey became the foundation of Pardesi Consultancy — a place built to offer honest guidance, clear explanations, and structured support.

Our mission is simple: help others move forward with confidence, using guidance shaped by real experience — not guesswork.
