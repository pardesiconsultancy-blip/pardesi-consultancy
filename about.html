WHO WE ARE

Supporting your academic ambitions.

Pardesi Consultancy is focused on helping students explore educational opportunities at Canadian colleges and universities.

We understand that selecting an institution and a program is an important decision. Every student has a different academic history, set of interests and future aspirations.

Our approach begins with understanding your educational profile. We then help you explore suitable institutions and programs and provide practical support as you navigate the admissions process.

Our goal is simple: make the process more understandable, organized and focused on your individual educational goals.
