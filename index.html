HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions.

A few helpful answers to get you started.

How can PARDESI help me?

We help students explore Canadian colleges and universities, understand educational program options and navigate admissions requirements and application steps.

Can you help me choose a college or university?

Yes. We can help you explore institutions and educational programs by considering your academic background, interests and goals.

What information should I prepare?

Your previous academic qualifications, transcripts, preferred field of study and educational goals are useful starting points.

Do you guarantee admission?

No. Admission decisions are made independently by the respective educational institutions. We provide educational guidance and admissions assistance.

How do I get started?

Visit our Contact page and reach out through the contact form, email or WhatsApp. We can discuss your educational interests and next steps.
